Energy wheel

Each energy overpowers the next two clockwise and deals 20% more damage to them.

Tips

  • Landing hits fills your energy. Taking hits fills it a little.
  • After a combo hits, press Special to cancel straight into it.
  • At 30% health you awaken once: faster, 30% stronger, half your energy refills.
  • Big ultimates beat small projectiles when they collide.
  • On a phone, buttons appear under the arena. Double-tap an arrow to dash.
  • Every 5 wins with one of your own fighters earns a skill point to upgrade them in Create.
  • Every time the rival AI loses, it gains a level and a point in a random stat. It gets tougher the more you win.
