How to win

  • Land hits to fill your energy bar. Taking hits fills it a little too.
  • Guard blocks most damage from the front.
  • At 30% health you awaken: faster, 30% stronger, and half your energy refills.
  • With a full bar, press Ultimate for a cinematic finisher.
